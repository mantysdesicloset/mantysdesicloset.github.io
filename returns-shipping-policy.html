Manty's Desi Closet — Shipping & Returns Policy

Last updated: 12-Aug-2026

Thank you for shopping with Manty's Desi Closet. This policy explains how we ship your order and what to do if something isn't right. If you have any questions, reach out any time — details are at the bottom of this page.

1. Shipping Policy

1.1 Where We Ship

We currently ship within Australia only. We hope to expand internationally in the future — follow us on Instagram @mantysdesicloset_official for updates.

1.2 Order Processing Time

Orders are carefully packed and dispatched within [1–3] business days of payment confirmation. During sale periods or festive seasons, processing may take a little longer — we'll let you know if there's a delay.

1.3 Delivery Timeframes

Once dispatched, orders typically arrive within:

  • Metro areas: 3–7 business days
  • Regional/remote areas: up to 10 business days

1.4 Shipping Costs

Calculated at checkout

1.5 Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation with a tracking number by email or SMS so you can follow your parcel's journey.

1.6 Delayed or Lost Parcels

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected timeframe, please contact us at hello.mantysdesicloset@gmail.com or +61 475 470 092 with your order number, and we'll investigate with the courier on your behalf.

2. Returns & Exchanges Policy

2.1 Change of Mind Returns

We want you to love what you ordered. If you've simply changed your mind, you may request a return within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and unaltered
  • In its original condition with all tags attached
  • Free of makeup, perfume, deodorant marks, or odours
  • Returned in its original packaging where possible

Return shipping is paid by the customer for change-of-mind returns.

2.2 Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and craftsmanship reasons, the following items cannot be returned for change of mind:

  • Innerwear, lingerie, and accessories worn close to the skin
  • Earrings and other pierced jewellery
  • Items marked "Final Sale" or purchased at clearance pricing
  • Made-to-order or custom-altered/tailored pieces

2.3 Faulty, Damaged, or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged in transit, or isn't what you ordered, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos of the issue. In this case:

  • We cover the cost of return shipping.
  • You may choose a replacement, exchange, or full refund.

This is in addition to — and does not limit — your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which guarantees a remedy for goods that are faulty, not fit for purpose, or significantly different from how they were described, regardless of any change-of-mind timeframe above.

2.4 How to Request a Return

  • Email hello.mantysdesicloset@gmail.com or message us on Instagram/WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return.
  • We'll confirm eligibility and provide a return address.
  • Post the item back to us in its original condition.
  • Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund or exchange.

2.5 Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within [3–7] business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item, back to your original payment method.

2.6 Exchanges

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your preferred size/style is unavailable, we'll offer a refund.
