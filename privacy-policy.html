Last updated: 12-Aug-2026

Manty's Desi Closet ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

As a small business, we may not yet be legally required to comply with the full APPs — but we've chosen to follow them as good practice, and this will become a legal requirement for small businesses from 10 December 2026, when the small business exemption is removed.
